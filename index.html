The four directories

Free. And most owners don't know they exist.

The State of New Jersey runs four directories where productions look for local vendors, crew, locations, and on-screen talent. Listing is free for any New Jersey business or resident.

  • NJ-411 Production Services Directory — businesses, vendors, suppliers
  • NJ-411 Crew Directory — individual crew and staff
  • Location Photo Library — properties productions can shoot at
  • NJ Actors Hub — on-screen-ready talent (launched April 2026)

Listing is free. Getting listed properly — with the keywords, photos, and descriptions productions actually search — is where most local businesses fall short. That's our work.

What changes in the production-ready version:

· Specialism leads — funeral flowers, scene by era — instead of "weddings and events"

· Three categories instead of one — visible to props supervisors and set decorators, not just caterers

· Production vocabulary — casket spray, prop-grade, COI, P-card, net-30 — signals you know the business

· Concrete capability commitments — 24-hour assembly, 90-minute callbacks, 50+ stem cooler

· Direct line + owner's name — no phone tree

· Photos that match the listing copy — what scouts confirm before calling
