What changes in the production-ready version:

· Specialism leads — funeral flowers, scene by era — instead of "weddings and events"

· Three categories instead of one — visible to props supervisors and set decorators, not just caterers

· Production vocabulary — casket spray, prop-grade, COI, P-card, net-30 — signals you know the business

· Concrete capability commitments — 24-hour assembly, 90-minute callbacks, 50+ stem cooler

· Direct line + owner's name — no phone tree

· Photos that match the listing copy — what scouts confirm before calling

Our focus

One focus. Four directories.

Production Ready NJ specializes in the NJ-411 Support Services Directory — the listing that gets vendors, suppliers, and local businesses found by productions. It's where the biggest opportunity is, where most owners don't know they should be listed, and where doing it right has the most impact.

The State of New Jersey runs three other directories I also know how to navigate:

  • Location Photo Library — if your space (restaurant, store, warehouse, hotel, B&B, distinctive home, parking lot, anything photogenic) could work as a film location.
  • NJ-411 Crew Directory — if you or someone on your team works in production: camera, lighting, sound, makeup, wardrobe, set decoration, transportation, props, post-production.
  • NJ Actors Hub — if you, your staff, or someone in your family is on-camera ready, including non-union work and background/extras.

If any of those apply, get in touch — these are handled case-by-case. The packages on this page cover Support Services listings only.
