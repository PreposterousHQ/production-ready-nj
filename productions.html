Notable stories

The kind of order most owners never see coming.

The $19,000 balloon order

A New Jersey balloon shop received a single-day order of $19,000 for a Billy Eichner production. The owner had no idea productions were even hiring from NJ until the call came in.

For: florists, party stores, anyone with bulk product.

The Cranford sub shop

Steven Soderbergh got hooked on Sub-Ology's spicy tuna sub while filming Presence in 2024. Production gave staff hats stitched with the film's title. Premiere catering: $1,200 order.

For: every deli, pizzeria, sandwich shop, sub shop owner in Monmouth.

Bahrs Landing becomes 1965 Newport

Owner Jay Cosgrove had his entire Highlands staff making omelets and pancakes all day in April 2024 — the Bahrs dining room dressed as a Newport diner for A Complete Unknown. Edward Norton (as Pete Seeger) and Nick Offerman (as Alan Lomax) filmed on site.

For: any waterfront, period-feel, or family-owned restaurant.

The Spielberg replica in Howell

In 2004, Spielberg built a replica of a destroyed suburban Howell street on Canterbury Way for War of the Worlds. Howell police directed traffic. The set is still on the Universal Studios Hollywood back-lot tour.

For: every owner who thinks their town doesn't qualify. It does.

The Freehold prop house

ACTION AC and Prop Rentals in Freehold had primarily served the NY industry for years. Working with Sandler's production on Happy Gilmore 2 was their first major in-state film job. Managing Director Elena Gonzalez said publicly the experience opened a new chapter for the company.

For: hardware stores, lumber yards, party rentals, equipment yards.

Why this matters for you

Productions hire inside the radius.

Productions don't bring their vendors with them. Crews source from a 30-mile zone around the set — and NJ tax credits explicitly incentivize spreading the spend across the state.

If you're in Howell, Freehold, Manalapan, Long Branch, Holmdel, Red Bank, or anywhere in Monmouth County, you're inside the radius for every shoot happening in our region. The vendors who get the call are the ones the scout can actually find.

That's what we do — make you findable.
